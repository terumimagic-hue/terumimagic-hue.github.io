Privacy Policy – MIKO Shorts Factory

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Who uses this app

MIKO Shorts Factory is used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Top Five Daily, to manage that owner's own channel. It is not available to the public.

What Google data the app uses

  • YouTube upload and channel access – to upload the owner's own videos, set their privacy status and publish time, and read their titles and status.
  • YouTube Analytics (read-only) – to read view and retention statistics of the owner's own videos.

How the data is stored and shared

  • The sign-in token and the statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account – Third-party connections. Locally stored data is deleted by the owner on request.

Contact

Through the Top Five Daily channel page on YouTube.
